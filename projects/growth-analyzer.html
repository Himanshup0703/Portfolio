Overview
What I Built

Growth Analyzer is a full-stack AI web application built to solve a real challenge: tracking skill development, career progress, and self-reflection in one intelligent platform. Inspired by GitHub's approach to tracking contributions, I applied the same philosophy to personal growth.

The platform features a personalized dashboard that surfaces patterns across a user's skills, projects, and reflections. Every entry is categorized by phase (School, College, Career) and scored, allowing the AI to surface growth trends and give actionable career analysis.

The Gemini-powered Job Portal takes a user's growth profile and matches it against live job listings from LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed — ranked by relevance with next-step guidance. This bridges self-awareness with real-world opportunity.

The interactive Growth Timeline lets users log achievements, tag skills, and filter entries — with an AI Career Analysis button that generates structured insights. Built with Next.js 16, TypeScript, MongoDB, and Recharts for visualization.
